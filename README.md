```
∂²ψₚᵃ/∂t² = c² · Δ_seq(ψ)ₚᵃ + c_chan² · Δ_chanᵃ(hₜ)
             - κ · ∂ψₚᵃ/∂t - ψₚᵃ
             - λ · K_{bc}^a · ψₚ^b ⊙ ψₚ^c
             + β · (ψₚᵃ - (ψₚ^a)³)
             + εₚ(t)
```

Where:

- `Δ_seq` is the Laplacian over the sequence dimension (adjacent tokens)
- `Δ_chan` is the Fano graph Laplacian over the 7 channels
- `c²` is the sequence propagation speed
- `c_chan²` is the channel coupling speed
- `εₚ(t)` is per-position noise

## Appendix: why n=7 breaks the hexanacci ladder

**Definitions.** A *hexanacci geometric ladder of order n* over an algebra `A`
is a pair `(M, v)` — an `n×n` matrix `M` over `A` and a nonzero seed vector
`v ∈ Aⁿ` — such that the sequence `w_k = M^k v` satisfies the hexanacci
recurrence componentwise:

```
w_k = w_{k-1} + w_{k-2} + w_{k-3} + w_{k-4} + w_{k-5} + w_{k-6},   k ≥ 6
```

and `v, Mv, M²v, …, M⁵v` are linearly independent (so the ladder actually
uses all `n` rungs — no degenerate collapse). This is equivalent to saying
the cyclic subspace generated by `v` under `M` has `M` acting as the
companion matrix of the hexanacci characteristic polynomial

```
p(x) = x⁶ − x⁵ − x⁴ − x³ − x² − x − 1
```

**Step 1 — the degree-6 ceiling (algebra-independent).** `p(x)` has degree 6.
For *any* unital algebra, the cyclic subspace `span(v, Mv, M²v, …)` generated
by a single seed has dimension exactly equal to the degree of the minimal
polynomial that `v` satisfies under `M`. If that minimal polynomial divides
`p(x)`, the cyclic subspace has dimension **at most 6**. So:

- For `n = 1..6`: take `M` to be the `n×n` truncation/embedding of the
  companion matrix of `p(x)` (or, for `n<6`, any invariant factor of it) — a
  genuine ladder exists. This is why "smaller sizes can."
- For `n = 7`: a ladder would require a 7-dimensional cyclic subspace, but
  the recurrence caps the minimal polynomial at degree 6. There is no room
  for a 7th linearly independent rung. This bound holds even over the reals,
  before octonions enter the picture at all.

**Step 2 — does non-associativity buy back the missing dimension?** This is
the only way `n = 7` could dodge Step 1: octonion multiplication is
non-associative, so in principle `M^k v` is ambiguous, and different
bracketings might reach outside the 6-dimensional span and manufacture a
genuine 7th direction.

Artin's theorem forecloses this: *the subalgebra generated by any two
elements of an alternative algebra (octonions included) is associative.*
`M` and `v` are two objects generating the whole ladder by repeated
multiplication, so every bracketing of `M^k v` lives inside a single
associative subalgebra. Within an associative subalgebra the Step 1 bound is
exact — there is no bracketing trick that escapes it. Non-associativity
cannot rescue the 7th dimension because the construction never actually
leaves associative territory.

**Step 3 — why 7 specifically, and not a coincidence to wave away.** The
octonions have exactly 7 independent imaginary units, indexed by the 7
points of the Fano plane; each of the 7 lines of the Fano plane spans an
associative quaternion subalgebra, but *no single quaternion subalgebra
contains all 7 imaginary directions* — that's precisely the combinatorial
content of the Fano plane (any two points determine a unique third, so two
generators only ever reach 3 of the 7 directions associatively). Step 2
already shows the ladder is confined to an associative (≤6-dimensional
before quaternion collapse, ≤6-dimensional by the recurrence regardless) 2-generator
subalgebra. So the two obstructions reinforce rather than cancel: the
recurrence caps the ladder at 6 rungs, and the octonions' own associativity
structure guarantees you can never legally recruit a 7th, independent
imaginary direction from just `M` and `v` to fill the gap.

**Conclusion.** A hexanacci geometric ladder exists for `n = 1, …, 6` (built
directly from the companion matrix of `p(x)`), and provably fails at
`n = 7` for two independent, mutually reinforcing reasons: the recurrence's
characteristic polynomial has degree 6 (an algebra-independent ceiling), and
Artin's theorem confines the octonionic construction to an associative
2-generator subalgebra where that ceiling is exact — the extra imaginary
direction that makes octonions 7-dimensional is exactly the direction the
construction is structurally barred from reaching.
